## What it is

A duel in the mould of *For Honor*, dropped into a *Rainbow Six Siege* round
structure. You set your guard high, left or right; so does your opponent. Every
exchange is decided by whether you read the incoming blade in time.

Rounds run attacker against defender with one life each, and sides swap every
round. The defender boards up doors and hardens walls during preparation; the
attacker blows holes in them with breach charges. Destroying a wall genuinely
changes how the building can be moved through — the opponent reroutes through
the holes you make.

## How to play

Set your guard with the mouse once you lock onto someone. Left click is a light
attack, right click a heavy — hold it to charge an unblockable. Time a heavy
against an incoming blade to parry it, and the attacker is left wide open. Miss
the timing and your own swing comes out instead.

`E` breaks a guard, `Space` dodges, `F` feints, `1`–`4` use your utility.

## Made of

Every model is generated by a Blender script rather than sculpted — six weapons,
six suits of armour and the whole compound, all procedural. Every sound is
synthesised in the browser at runtime; there is not a single audio file.

Reach is measured rather than tuned: each fighter runs its own rig through every
attack animation on load and derives, from where the blade actually travels, how
far it reaches and the exact instant it crosses a target. That is why six very
different weapons all work on one set of animations.
