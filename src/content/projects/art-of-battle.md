## What it is

A sword fight in the style of *For Honor*, set inside a round structure
borrowed from *Rainbow Six Siege*. You hold your guard high, left or right; so
does your opponent. Every exchange comes down to whether you read the incoming
blade in time.

Rounds are attacker against defender with one life each, and the sides swap
every round. During the prep phase the defender boards up doors and reinforces
walls; the attacker blows holes in them with breach charges. Destroying a wall
really does change how the building can be moved through — your opponent
reroutes through the holes you make.

## How to play

Set your guard with the mouse once you have locked onto someone. Left click is
a light attack, right click a heavy — hold it to charge an unblockable one.
Time a heavy against an incoming blade to parry it, and the attacker is left
wide open. Miss the timing and your own swing comes out instead.

`E` breaks a guard, `Space` dodges, `F` feints, `1`–`4` use your gadgets.

## How it was built

Every model is generated by a Blender script rather than sculpted by hand — six
weapons, six suits of armour and the whole building, all made in code. Every
sound is generated in the browser as it plays; there is not a single audio file.

Reach is measured, not tuned by hand. On load, each fighter runs its own rig
through every attack animation and works out, from where the blade actually
travels, how far it reaches and the exact moment it crosses a target. That is
why six very different weapons all work on one set of animations.
